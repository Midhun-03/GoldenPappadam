# Golden Pappadam

The language of a family pappadam manufacturer in Kundara, Kollam, which sells to shops on credit through salesmen on van routes and an office.

## Language

### Pricing

**Agreed rate**:
The price one customer pays for one product, by arrangement with that customer. Every branch of the customer pays it. It is never zero: a customer with no agreed rate pays the standard price.
_Avoid_: Customer price, special price

**Standard price**:
A product's own wholesale price, paid by any customer with no agreed rate for it. Every product has one, and it is never zero.
_Avoid_: MRP, default price, selling price

**Shop's rate**:
The price a customer pays for a product right now: its agreed rate if it has one, otherwise the standard price.
_Avoid_: Price, current price

**Last-known rate**:
The shop's rate as it stood when the salesman's phone last heard from the office. A bill made away from the office is priced at it.

### Discounts

**Discount**:
An amount in rupees taken off the whole of a bill at the moment it is made. Settling an old bill for less than it is worth is not a discount.
_Avoid_: Rebate, settlement discount, item discount

**Discount cap**:
The largest discount a salesman may give, as a share of the bill's value before discount and tax. The office is not bound by it.

**Discount reason**:
The salesman's own words for why a discount was given, kept for the office and never shown to the shop.
